# AVT FLOWER MERCHANT — FUTURISTIC DARK + GLASSMORPHISM SPECIFICATION
**Identity Direction:** "Futuristic Dark + Glassmorphism (Cyber-Botanical)"
**Designed by:** AI Software Company Design Division (Head of Design 05, Brand Designer 05e, UI Designer 05b, UX Writer 05f)

---

## 1. Aesthetic Philosophy: "The Cyber-Botanical Market"
This style combines the intense speed of financial trading desks with the high-tech elegance of futuristic sci-fi interfaces:
- **Atmospheric Canvas:** Infinite deep space canvas (`#070a12` to `#0d1322`) illuminated with subtle radial aurora orbs (emerald `#10b981` and cyan `#06b6d4`).
- **Frosted Glass Panels:** `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.72)` with hardware-accelerated `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and polished border bevels `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.09)`.
- **Luminous Cyber Accents:**
  - Electric Cyan (`#06b6d4` / `#22d3ee`) for focus states and lot identification.
  - Neon Emerald (`#10b981` / `#34d399`) for profits, active statuses, and primary executions.
  - Solar Marigold (`#f59e0b` / `#fbbf24`) for auction warnings and commission highlights.
  - Deep Violet Glow (`#6366f1` / `#8b5cf6`) for net settlement balances.
- **Tabular Data HUD:** Pure high-contrast `JetBrains Mono` figures with subtle neon text shadows.
